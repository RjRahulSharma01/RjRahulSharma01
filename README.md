### From newsroom to repo

I studied science in school, then journalism, then spent 13 years in content, social and digital marketing. For most of that time I wrote the brief and someone else built the thing.

Now I build it myself. Journalism taught me to ask what the reader actually needs; marketing taught me to measure whether it worked. Those two habits turn out to be most of what makes a useful tool. AI pair-programming closed the rest of the gap.
